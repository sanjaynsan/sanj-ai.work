About

Sanjay is an architect working at the intersection of design, making, and emerging technologies, holding a Master in City and Technology (MaCT) from IAAC Barcelona. With 2 years of experience in architecture and computational design, Sanjay has worked across a range of projects from concept development to detailed design and execution, with a focus on spatial clarity, material logic, and buildability. Sanjay's academic and professional background is complemented by a strong engagement with advanced design methodologies, where computational tools support and extend architectural thinking. Sanjay maintains a focused interest in fabrication and prototyping, exploring the relationship between digital workflows and physical making through CNC machining, 3D printing, and robotic processes, with his work driven by the intent to produce precise, well considered architecture while advancing methods of design and construction through continuous experimentation.
