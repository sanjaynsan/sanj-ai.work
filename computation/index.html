About

An enthusiast at the intersection of design and intelligence, I recently graduated with a MaCT (Masters in City and Technology) from IAAC Barcelona, where I was honored with the "Advanced Urban Computation Excellence" award for the batch and served on the IAAC research team. With a Bachelor's in Architecture and over 4+ years of experience in computational design and AI, I develop advanced computational design technologies for complex urban systems and the built environment. My passion lies in transforming raw data into design intelligence by generating, extracting, collecting, and articulating insights that drive innovative, multidisciplinary solutions and multitech stacked automation workflows.
